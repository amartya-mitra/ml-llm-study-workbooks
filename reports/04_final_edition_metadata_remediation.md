## 1. The contradiction

The canonical PDF `outputs/04-modern-llm-architecture-workbook.pdf`
(70 pages, SHA-256 `f1a89195…4e048`) said "Final Edition" on its title page
but, on page 5, carried a callout titled **"Complete content draft —

the final edition", narrated the RC2–RC7 correction history, and called it

called it "a review artifact". The Build and Version Note on the last pages
also said "RC1–RC7 were release-candidate drafts" and claimed the content
had been "copy-edited".
